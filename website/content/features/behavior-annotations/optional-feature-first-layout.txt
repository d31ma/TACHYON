Role annotations require a type suffix, exact matching filename and immediate
plural role folder. One primary role type belongs in each file. Modifiers
(CacheSettings, Setting, Table, CatalogSettings, etc.) do not rename the type.
Role files are discovered in their realm's shared role folders:
client/<role>s for Tac and server/<role>s for Yon; only controllers
stay beside their route. Unannotated
helpers do not register or create a global container. Ordinary unannotated
helpers remain ordinary code; native Tac keeps its service type first.
See the Strict annotation layout guide for all supported languages.
